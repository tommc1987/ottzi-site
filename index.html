Meet Ask Otto

Like texting a friend who really knows dogs.

Otto is our black-and-white pup, and he lives in the app. Ask him about food, training, behaviour or the odd 3am "is this normal?" worry, and he'll answer in plain English.

  • Remembers your dog's quirks, like a sensitive tummy or a fear of the hoover
  • You can see and delete what Otto remembers at any time
  • Tells you straight away when something needs a vet instead
Weight & nutriment

Know if they're on track.

Log a weigh-in now and then and Ottzi plots it against the healthy range for your dog's breed and size, so slow changes stand out long before they become a problem. Nutriment builds a daily plan for food, water, exercise and supplements around your dog.

  • Weight trend against a healthy range for their size
  • Daily food, water and calorie guide, personalised
  • Walk tracking with route, distance and time
Wellness Scan · Premium

A quick once-over, any time you like.

Take a photo of your dog and Ottzi checks the everyday things owners often miss, like a dull coat, weepy eyes or long nails. It suggests simple next steps and keeps a history so you can compare over time.

  • Plain-English results with practical tips
  • Scan history saved, so changes are easy to see
  • Honest by design: we'll always point you to a vet if it matters

Good questions

Things people ask us.

Is Ottzi really free?

Yes. The care diary, Ask Otto, walks, weight and reminders are all free. Ottzi Premium (£2.39/month or £23.99/year) adds extras like Wellness Scan, and you never have to upgrade.

Does Ask Otto replace my vet?

No, and he'll tell you that himself. Otto is great for everyday questions and peace of mind. If something sounds like it needs a professional, he'll say so and point you to your vet.

Can I add more than one pet?

Yes. Add every dog in the house (cats too) and each one gets their own diary, weight history and reminders.

How do I cancel Premium?

Just like any other app subscription. On iPhone, go to Settings → your name → Subscriptions. On Android, open Google Play → Payments & subscriptions. There are no phone calls and no hoops to jump through.

I've got a GOODBOY code from my hamper. How do I use it?

Download Ottzi, then redeem the code through the App Store or Google Play to unlock a month of Premium. Step-by-step instructions are at ottoandfern.com/app.

What happens to my data?

Your dog's info is used to run the app and nothing else. We don't sell it and there are no ads. You can see and delete what Otto remembers, or delete all your data, from your account screen.
